## 🚰 Bike Hydration Setup

| Bottle | Location | Contents | Size |
|--------|----------|----------|------|
| Aero bottle | Between aerobars | LMNT #1 mixed in | 20–28oz |
| Cage bottle | Down tube (already have) | LMNT #2 mixed in | 32oz |
| Aid stations | — | Grab plain water, pour into aero bottle when empty | — |

**Flow:**
1. Drink aero bottle (LMNT #1) first half of ride
2. When empty, pour cage bottle into aero bottle OR drink from cage bottle directly
3. Refill with plain water from aid stations as needed

**What to buy:** Just the aero bottle — downtube cage is already covered.
Optional: behind-the-seat cage (~$15–25) as a 3rd bottle buffer, but not required for 70.3.
Brands: Profile Design or Xlab for aero bottles and rear cages.
