## Course Description
Foundational accounting course covering financial accounting and reporting concepts. Topics include:
- Preparation of income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement
- Analysis and recording of business transactions
- Accounting for assets, liabilities, and equity

## Learning Outcomes
- Apply financial accounting concepts to qualitative and quantitative problems
- Demonstrate understanding of financial accounting and decision making
- Improve critical thinking skills
- Collaborative/team-based learning
- Expand Excel skills through project-based assignments
