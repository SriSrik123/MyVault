# Course Syllabus — CS-270
**Date:** 2026-03-31
**Source:** Course Information – CS-270-003 - SP 25-26

## Overview
CS-270: Mathematical Foundations in Computer Science. Spring 2025-26, 3 credits. Instructor: Prof. Joseph A. Gallego Mejia. Meets Tue/Thu 3:00–4:20 PM at 3675 Market Street, Room 1103. The course covers formal logic, natural deduction, predicates, Racket programming, recursion, mathematical induction, and NP-completeness. TAs hold office hours in the CLC (3675 Market St, Room 1066).

## Key Concepts

**Topics Covered (10 weeks)**
- Formal Logic & Natural Deduction (Wk 1–2)
- Disjunction Elimination, Proofs by Contradiction (Wk 2)
- Predicates and First-Order Logic (Wk 3; University Holiday Monday Apr 13)
- Introduction to Racket; Recursion and Lists (Wk 4)
- Equational Reasoning; Higher-Order Functions (Wk 5)
- Midterm Review + **Midterm** (Wk 6 — in class, second lecture = Thu May 7)
- Mathematical Induction, Peano Arithmetic (Wk 7)
- Induction on Lists, Lists as Trees (Wk 8)
- Boolean Normal Forms, Expressions as Trees (Wk 9; Memorial Day May 25)
- SAT Solvers, NP-Complete Problems (Wk 10)

**Assignments**
- Weekly Homework (HW 1–9): due **Friday 11:59 PM** each week.
- Labs: completed in-class at the end of each lecture session, due same day.
- Two late passes available — each grants 1 extra day on any homework (must request before deadline).

## Summary
CS-270 blends formal logic with functional programming (Racket), culminating in computational complexity (NP). Keeping up with weekly homework is critical; labs happen each class period. The midterm is in-class during the second lecture of Week 6 (Thursday, May 7). Office hours are in the CLC and are the best resource for homework support.
