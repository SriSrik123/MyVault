# Business Meetings During the Term — COM-270
**Date:** 2026-04-06
**Source:** COM 270 Lecture-Business meetings.pdf (Week 2)

## Overview
This lecture introduces the Business Meeting assignment structure for COM 270-905. Over the course of the term, students conduct business meetings during Weeks 2, 4, 6, and 8, each focused on tracking and presenting news about a chosen business topic. Because the course is online, meetings take the form of asynchronous discussion posts rather than synchronous presentations.

## Key Concepts

### Business Meeting Structure
- Meetings occur in **Weeks 2, 4, 6, and 8**
- Each student/group picks a **business topic** — could be a company (e.g., Intel), an issue (e.g., trade with Finland), or a product/service
- In each meeting, you **report news from the previous two weeks** about your topic
- Posts go early in the week so classmates can reply and discuss

### Setting Up a News Aggregator
- Use **Google News** or another RSS/news aggregator to track your topic
- Search "how to set up a news feed" for setup instructions
- Keeping up with the news continuously is key to presenting well

### Assignments & Grading
- Present your work as a **recorded PowerPoint** (or accessible presentation format)
- Slideshows are posted to a Blackboard discussion
- Classmates ask follow-up questions and engage in discussion
- **Grades** are based on quality of presentation AND quality of discussion participation
- A general discussion list will be available for process questions

### Modes of Communication (Meta-thoughts)
- Online format loses **synchronous immediacy** but gains **temporal flexibility**
- In a face-to-face version, a moderator would be needed; only one person presents at a time
- Think critically about the **affordances** of the communicative environment — what each mode offers and what it limits
- This concept of "affordances" is a key term for the course

## Important Definitions
- **Affordances** — the capabilities and constraints that a particular communicative environment offers (e.g., async discussion allows flexible timing but lacks real-time back-and-forth)
- **Asynchronous discussion** — posts made and responded to at different times, as opposed to synchronous (real-time) communication

## Summary
The business meeting assignments are a recurring, discussion-based exercise where you track real-world business news and present it to your classmates. The online format makes this asynchronous, so preparation (setting up a news feed, posting early) is essential. A key meta-theme of the course is critically examining what different communication modes afford and constrain.
