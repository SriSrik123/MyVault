# Course Syllabus — COM-270
**Date:** 2026-03-31
**Source:** COM 270-905 Syllabus(1)

## Overview
COM-270: Business Communication (Section 905). Spring 2025-26. Instructor: Dr. Wesley Shumar (shumarw@drexel.edu; 3201 Arch St, Suite 340, Rm 372). Fully asynchronous, online course. Covers professional writing (letters, resumes, memos, proposals) and oral communication (presentations, interviews) in a business context. Writing-intensive with weekly discussions for attendance tracking.

## Key Concepts

**Course Topics (10 weeks)**
- Week 1: Disseminating Information
- Week 2: Principles of Communication
- Week 3: Written Communication → **Resume & Job Letter Assignment Due**
- Week 4: Transactional Communication
- Week 5: Interactional Communication → **Midterm Exam**
- Week 6: Conflict and Social Control
- Week 7: Leadership
- Week 8: Persuasion
- Week 9: Vision
- Finals Week: **Final Exam / Final Project (Business Proposal)**

**Participation**
- Weekly discussions posted on Blackboard — participation is how "attendance" is tracked.
- Contact professor if emergencies prevent weekly participation; excused absences case-by-case.

**Textbooks**
- Sullivan, J. (2017). *Simply Said: Communication Better at Work and Beyond*. Wiley.
- Coven, M. B. (2022). *Writing on the Job: Best Practices for Communicating in the Digital Age*. Princeton UP.
- LibreTexts *Communication for Business Success* (free, online, recommended).

**AI Policy**: Educational use of AI (grammar, understanding concepts) is allowed with citation; using AI to write entire assignments is considered academic dishonesty.

## Summary
COM-270 is an async writing course focused on practical business communication skills. The three major grades (Midterm, Presentation, Final Proposal) are each 30% — writing quality and professional tone are paramount. Resume/Job Letter is due around Week 3; stay current with weekly Blackboard discussions to maintain "attendance." All exams are take-home essays; the Final Exam is a full business proposal.
