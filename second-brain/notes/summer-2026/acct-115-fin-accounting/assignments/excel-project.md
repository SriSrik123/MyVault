## Objective
Record journal entries, adjustments, and create financial statements.

## Method
Slideshow of a hypothetical business:
- **Title Slide**: business name, partner names, picture of business type
- **Body Slide 1 & 2**: 10 journal entries during the month (copy/paste or screenshot from Excel), must cover Cash, A/R, A/P, Prepaid Expense, Deferred Revenue, Revenue, Expense, each with a description
- **Body Slide 3**: 4 adjusting entries at month end: deferred revenue, deferred expense, accrued revenue, accrued expense, each with a description
- **Body Slide 4**: Financial statements (copy/paste or screenshot from Excel): income statement + post-closing trial balance
