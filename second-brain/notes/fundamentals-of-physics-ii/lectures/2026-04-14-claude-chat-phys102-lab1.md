## Notes / Things to remember
- AC current used (not DC) to avoid electrode polarization
- Distilled water = conducting medium (not tap water)
- Part B max field was **100 N/C** between the 7–9 V pair, much higher than Part A's ~28.6 N/C because the ring concentrates field lines more than a flat strip does
- The 5 V equipotential in Part A was nearly a straight vertical line — confirms bilateral symmetry of the two-ring dipole geometry
