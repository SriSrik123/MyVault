# Course Syllabus — PHYS-102
**Date:** 2026-03-31
**Source:** Personal — Syllabus – PHYS-102-A - SP 25-26

## Overview
PHYS-102: Fundamentals of Physics II — Electricity and Magnetism. Spring 2026. Three-credit lecture course with required recitation and lab. Covers electrostatics, circuits, magnetism, and electromagnetic induction. Instructor: Prof. Som Tyagi (Sections A & B); Prof. Bryan VanSaders (Section C).

## Key Concepts

**Course Structure**
- **Lectures**: introduce concepts and worked examples.
- **Recitations** (required): group problem-solving with TA; includes a 20-minute Group Activity at the end of each session.
- **Laboratories** (4 total): hands-on experiments. Labs begin Week 2. Even-numbered sections attend even-numbered weeks; odd sections attend odd-numbered weeks. Makeup labs in Week 10.

**Assignments**
- Conceptual Exercises: due every **Friday 11:59 PM** on Drexel Learn (multiple-choice, concept-check).
- General Numerical Problems (ACHIEVE): due every **Monday 11:59 PM**.
- Textbook: Halliday/Resnick/Krane (available via ACHIEVE on Drexel Learn).

**Lab Experiments**
1. Electric Fields and Electric Potentials
2. Electrostatic Force between Charged Parallel Plates
3. Direct Current Circuits – Resistors and Capacitors
4. Motion of charged particles in E and B Fields
